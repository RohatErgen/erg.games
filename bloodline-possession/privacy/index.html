Summary

Bloodline Possession ("the Game") is a mobile game for Android made by Erg Digital Studio ("we", "us"). The Game has no accounts or sign-up, and we do not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or precise location.

  • Your game progress is saved on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

The Game shows optional rewarded ads (watch an ad to get an in-game reward) and a short ad at the end of a run. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and approximate location to show relevant ads and measure them.

  • Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads.
  • The "No Ads" purchase removes the end-of-run ad and lets you claim ad rewards without watching.

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms. We only receive the confirmation needed to deliver your item and to restore permanent purchases if you reinstall the Game. We never receive or store your card or payment information.

Data deletion

Your game progress lives on your device: uninstalling the Game deletes it.

To request deletion of any other data related to you, e-mail ergdigitaldev@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion – Bloodline Possession". We reply and delete the data within 30 days. Data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google; you can reset your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.
